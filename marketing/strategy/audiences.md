# Audiences and learning jobs

The primary segmentation is the learner's job, not age, company size, or
purchase intent.

## 1. Curious beginner

**Job:** Understand what AI is without being sold a tool.

**Needs:** plain language, everyday examples, limits, vocabulary, one safe
first exercise.

**Best entry:** `/einstieg`, then `/wie-ki-funktioniert`.

## 2. Employee using AI at work

**Job:** Use AI without leaking data or forwarding unreliable output.

**Needs:** data classes, task boundaries, verification, human responsibility,
escalation.

**Best entry:** `/ki-fuehrerschein`.

## 3. Manager or operational owner

**Job:** Decide where AI is appropriate and what competence the team needs.

**Needs:** role-based literacy, risk context, documentation, realistic limits,
worker participation.

**Best entry:** `/ki-check`, `/eu-ai-act-kurs`, and the German learning path.

## 4. Educator

**Job:** Teach AI as knowledge, practice, judgment, and participation.

**Needs:** reusable lesson units, answer notes, sources, age adaptation,
licensing, misinformation exercises.

**Best entry:** `/ki-und-gesellschaft`, books, and future OER packs.

## 5. Self-employed professional

**Job:** Improve bounded tasks without buying a complex stack.

**Needs:** low-cost workflows, privacy checks, tool-neutral examples, honest
tradeoffs.

**Best entry:** `/buecher` and the core path.

## 6. Technical practitioner

**Job:** Build or evaluate AI-enabled systems with better engineering
judgment.

**Needs:** code, architecture, reproducible labs, local tooling, source and
license clarity.

**Best entry:** the six English deep-dive courses and `/open-source`.

## 7. Skeptical or affected citizen

**Job:** Understand where AI affects decisions and how to challenge unreliable
or unfair use.

**Needs:** rights, labor effects, manipulation, bias, provenance, synthetic
media, routes to primary sources.

**Best entry:** `/ki-und-gesellschaft` and the public blog.

## Excluded segmentation

Do not create content for "leads," "decision makers with budget," or funnel
stages. A learner can later become a contributor, teacher, or returning reader.
They do not become a sales opportunity inside this system.
