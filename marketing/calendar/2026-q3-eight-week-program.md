# Eight-week editorial program

Window: 27 July to 20 September 2026.

Core thesis: **KI verstehen. Sicher anwenden. Kritisch prüfen. Offen
mitgestalten.**

Dates are sequencing targets, not permission to bypass review.

## Weekly production sequence

### Monday

- Confirm learner job and canonical route.
- Refresh primary sources.
- Record legal and technical status date.
- Decide whether a stronger external resource should be linked instead.

### Tuesday

- Complete article draft.
- Run factual, license, accessibility, and limitation review.

### Wednesday

- Implement the canonical article through the website publication contract.
- Run content, structured-data, route, and browser checks.

### Thursday

- Publish the evidence-led LinkedIn post.
- Prepare the Page record and native visual or demonstration.

### Friday

- Resolve substantive corrections.
- Update the source and derivatives together.
- Record what changed in the public changelog.

## Dependencies

- Week 2 requires a same-day check of the Commission AI-literacy Q&A and German
  implementation status.
- Week 5 requires tested model, license, hardware, and data-boundary details.
- Week 7 requires explicit reuse rights and education metadata.
- Week 8 requires a verified coverage map from canonical platform registries.
