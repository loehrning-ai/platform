# LinkedIn post template

## Metadata

- Canonical source:
- Author:
- Intended reader:
- One outcome:
- Source review date:
- License:

## Draft

**Claim**

One direct sentence.

**Evidence**

Name the source, date, and relevant finding. Keep separate surveys separate.

**Consequence**

Explain what changes for the reader.

**Action**

Read, test, verify, correct, reuse, teach, or contribute.

## Review

- The post stands on its own.
- The derivative does not overstate the canonical source.
- The link points to the canonical resource.
- The opening is not engagement bait.
- No attention tagging or reaction voting.
- Hashtags are relevant and limited.
- Image alt text is prepared when media is used.
