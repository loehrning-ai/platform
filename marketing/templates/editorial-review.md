# Editorial review

## Reader

- Audience and prerequisite are explicit.
- The learning outcome is testable.
- The route to the next useful resource is clear.

## Claims

- Central claims have primary sources.
- Survey sample, population, field date, and publisher remain attached.
- Law, official proposal, guidance, and inference are labeled separately.
- Product status and counts match canonical registries.
- Experience is not generalized as evidence.

## Practice

- The exercise uses synthetic or non-sensitive data.
- The human decision boundary is explicit.
- The verification step can falsify the expected result.
- Failure and escalation are explained.

## Publication

- Dates and versions are present.
- Canonical URL and structured data are aligned.
- License and attribution are explicit.
- Third-party rights are documented.
- Correction route and change handling are present.
- Material AI assistance is disclosed.

## Language

- German `du` form outside legal pages.
- Concrete roles and examples.
- No hype, urgency, sales framing, or implied accreditation.
- No conclusion that exceeds the evidence.
