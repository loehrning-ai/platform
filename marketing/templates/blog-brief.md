## Structure

1. Direct answer.
2. Mental model.
3. Worked example.
4. Practical exercise.
5. Verification step.
6. What this does not prove.
7. Deeper resources.
8. Sources, license, and correction route.

## Rejection checks

- No unsupported superlative.
- No compliance or recognition promise.
- No pending feature presented as live.
- No personal or confidential data in the exercise.
- No third-party material without reuse rights.
- No hardcoded product count that can come from a registry.
