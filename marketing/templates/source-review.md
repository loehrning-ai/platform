## Reliability

- Is this the operative primary source?
- Does it link to the underlying law, dataset, research, or source code?
- Is the date current for the claim?
- Are the sample and method visible?
- Does the source distinguish fact, proposal, guidance, and opinion?
- Can the claim be checked independently?

## Claim extraction

Record the narrow claim supported by the source. Do not copy the source's
marketing interpretation.

## Limitations
