Workshop 04 · skeleton

ESG Reporting
with AI.

One question, held fixed

How much did our electricity emissions change from 2024 to 2025?

Raw inputs One checked table A clear statement

75 minutes · synthetic teaching case

Three files. One reported number.

Every ESG figure starts as a pile of files. Name each one before you ask an AI anything.

  1. Invoices

    12 PDFs from the utility, one per month

    Jan 2025: 38,410 kWh

  2. Meter export

    One CSV from facilities

    2025 total: 452,300 kWh

  3. Emission factor

    From the supplier contract

    0.366 kg CO₂e per kWh

452,300 kWh × 0.366 = 165.5 t CO₂e

Synthetic numbers. The invoices and the meter disagree by 1.2 %: write down which one you used.

Same inputs. Two summaries.

First AI draft

“Thanks to our green initiatives, emissions fell sharply this year.”

  • No number
  • No scope, no method
  • No source file
Cannot be checked
After a checked table

“Scope 2 (market-based) fell from 181.2 t to 165.5 t CO₂e, −8.7 %, from 2024 to 2025.”

  • Meter export, 452,300 kWh
  • Supplier factor, 0.366 kg per kWh
  • Calculation kept next to the claim
Every number has a source

Take this home.

  1. Name the source file behind every number.
  2. Say which scope, which method and which year.
  3. Keep the calculation next to the claim.

A checked table beats a fluent paragraph.
