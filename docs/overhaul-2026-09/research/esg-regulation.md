## 1. "What is true today" slide (as of 26 Sep 2026)

1. **Fewer companies must report.** Since Omnibus I (Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force since 18 Mar 2026), CSRD covers EU companies with **more than 1,000 employees and more than €450m net turnover**. Both conditions must be met.
2. **The standards got shorter.** Revised ESRS were published in the EU Official Journal on **21 Sep 2026** (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563). The Commission says they cut mandatory datapoints by more than 60% and all datapoints by more than 70%. They are mandatory for financial years starting on or after **1 Jan 2027**, and companies may apply them early for FY2026.
3. **Smaller companies are shielded.** Firms with up to 1,000 employees can refuse information requests from reporting customers that go beyond a voluntary standard, which is based on the VSME. That standard was also published on 21 Sep 2026 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560).
4. **Assurance stays "limited".** The route to reasonable assurance was removed. The Commission must adopt a limited-assurance standard by **1 Jul 2027**.
5. **Due diligence (CSDDD) is narrower and later.** It now applies to companies with more than 5,000 employees and more than €1.5bn turnover. Member states must transpose it by 26 Jul 2028, and companies must comply from **Jul 2029**. The duty to *adopt* a climate transition plan was removed.
6. **Germany had still not transposed the CSRD in the latest sources found.** The bill went through a Bundestag committee hearing on 13 Apr 2026. Final passage is **UNVERIFIED**.
7. **Carbon accounting rules are being rewritten, slowly.** GHG Protocol and ISO plan one merged corporate standard: consultation in Q2 2027, publication in Q4 2028. Today's rules still apply: the 2004 Corporate Standard, the 2011 Scope 3 Standard, and the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance with its dual location-based and market-based reporting.
8. **Greenwashing rules apply from tomorrow.** The Empowering Consumers Directive (EU) 2024/825 applies from **27 Sep 2026**. The separate Green Claims Directive is stalled.

### 2.1 EU CSRD after Omnibus I

**The legal act**
- Directive (EU) 2026/470 of 24 Feb 2026 amends Directives 2006/43/EC, 2013/34/EU, (EU) 2022/2464 (CSRD) and (EU) 2024/1760 (CSDDD).
  Sources: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/470/oj/eng (act dated 2026-02-24); https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ%3AL_202600470
- The Council gave final approval on **24 Feb 2026**.
  Source: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/24/council-signs-off-simplification-of-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-requirements-to-boost-eu-competitiveness/ (2026-02-24)
- It was published in the OJ on **26 Feb 2026** and entered into force on **18 Mar 2026**.
  Sources: https://finance.ec.europa.eu/publications/omnibus-i-package-commission-simplifies-rules-sustainability-and-eu-investments-delivering-over-eu6_en; https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/blogs/environment-health-safety-and-product-compliance/2026/eu-omnibus-i-directive-amending-csrd-and-csddd-will-enter-into-force-on-18-march-2026 (secondary, 2026-03)
- **Transposition deadlines:** Articles 1–3 (accounting, audit and CSRD changes) by **19 Mar 2027**; Article 4 (CSDDD) by **26 Jul 2028**.
  Source: EUR-Lex text of 2026/470 as indexed (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2026/470/oj/eng)

- **Wave-1 relief:** member states may exempt wave-1 companies (reporting since FY2024) that fall below the new thresholds, for FY2025 and FY2026. https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/24/council-signs-off-simplification-of-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-requirements-to-boost-eu-competitiveness/ (2026-02-24)

### 2.2 The 2025 "stop-the-clock" Directive (EU) 2025/794

- The Council adopted it on **14 Apr 2025**. It was published in the OJ on **16 Apr 2025** and entered into force on 17 Apr 2025. https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/04/14/simplification-council-gives-final-green-light-on-the-stop-the-clock-mechanism-to-boost-eu-competitiveness-and-provide-legal-certainty-to-businesses/ (2025-04-14); https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ%3AL_202500794 ; https://www.garrigues.com/en_GB/new/stop-clock-directive-published-official-journal-european-union (secondary)

### 2.4 VSME and the value-chain cap

- **VSME (EFRAG):** released in Dec 2024. It has a **Basic Module of 11 disclosures**, including Scope 1 and 2 GHG, and a **Comprehensive Module of 9 further disclosures**. It requires no double materiality assessment and no assurance. https://www.efrag.org/en/news-and-calendar/news/efrag-releases-the-voluntary-sustainability-reporting-standard-for-nonlisted-smes ; https://www.efrag.org/en/smes-and-sustainability-reporting
- **Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1710 of 30 Jul 2025** recommends the VSME to non-listed SMEs and micro-undertakings, for answering data requests from banks and large customers. https://finance.ec.europa.eu/publications/commission-presents-voluntary-sustainability-reporting-standard-ease-burden-smes_en ; https://sustainablefutures.linklaters.com/post/102kyuo/european-commission-adopts-recommendation-on-voluntary-sustainability-reporting-f (secondary)
- **Value-chain cap (in 2026/470):** companies with **up to 1,000 employees** can refuse requests for information beyond what the voluntary standard contains. https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251211IPR32164/simplified-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-rules-for-businesses (2025-12); https://accountancyeurope.eu/publications/omnibus-explained-key-changes-to-the-csrd-and-csddd/
- **The binding voluntary standard:** **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560 of 3 Jul 2026**, "sustainability reporting standards for voluntary use by undertakings protected by the value chain cap". It is based on the VSME, was published in the OJ on **21 Sep 2026**, and entered into force on the third day after publication. Secondary sources say voluntary use is available **from 24 Sep 2026**. **Article 3 applies from FY2027.** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/1560/oj/eng ; https://www.efrag.org/en/news-and-calendar/news/european-commission-publishes-delegated-act-on-revised-esrs-and-voluntary-sustainability-reporting ; https://sustainablefutures.linklaters.com/post/102o1ou/eu-csrd-revised-esrs-and-voluntary-reporting-standard-are-published-in-the-offic (secondary)
- EFRAG calls the new standard the "Voluntary Standard" (VS) and has updated its guidance. https://www.efrag.org/en/news-and-calendar/news/european-commission-adopts-revised-esrs-and-voluntary-sustainability-reporting-standard-efrag

### 2.5 EU Taxonomy reporting simplification

- The Commission adopted the Taxonomy simplification act on **4 Jul 2025**. It was published as **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/73** in the OJ on **8 Jan 2026** and applies from **1 Jan 2026**. It covers reporting in 2026 on FY2025, with an option to apply the old rules for FY2025 and start with FY2026. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/73/oj/eng ; https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202600073 ; https://www.iasplus.com/en/news/2026/01/taxonomy-simplification-oj (secondary)

### 2.9 GHG Protocol

**Rules in force today**
- The **Corporate Standard (2004, revised edition)** defines three scopes. Scope 1 is direct emissions from owned or controlled sources. Scope 2 is indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heat and cooling. Scope 3 is all other indirect value-chain emissions. It covers seven gases: CO2, CH4, N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6 and NF3. https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-standard ; https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/ghg-protocol-revised.pdf
- The **Scope 3 Standard (2011)** has **15 categories**, upstream and downstream. https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-value-chain-scope-3-standard
- The **Scope 2 Guidance (2015)** requires **dual reporting**. The **location-based** method uses the average emission intensity of the grids where energy is consumed. The **market-based** method uses the company's contractual instruments, such as supplier-specific rates, energy attribute certificates and PPAs. https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2023-03/Scope%202%20Guidance.pdf ; https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/top-ten-questions-about-scope-2-guidance

**Revisions under way**
- **Scope 2 consultation:** open **20 Oct 2025 – 31 Jan 2026**. It proposed **hourly matching** of contractual instruments, **deliverability** requirements, updates to the location-based method, feasibility measures (load profiles, exemptions for smaller organisations, a legacy clause) and a phased rollout. https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/release-ghg-protocol-opens-public-consultations-scope-2-and-electricity-sector-consequential ; https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/upcoming-scope-2-public-consultation-hourly-matching-and-deliverability ; https://ghgprotocol.org/ghg-protocol-public-consultations
- The **feedback summary (29 Jul 2026)** reports about 1,100 responses from 56 countries and divergent views. GHG Protocol is now exploring "multiple reporting approaches". https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/S2-PublicConsultationSummaryofFeedback-2026.07.29.pdf ; https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/ghg-protocol-announces-key-standard-development-updates
- **ISO partnership:** announced **9 Sep 2025**. https://www.iso.org/news/2025/09/iso-and-ghgp-partnership ; https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/release-iso-and-ghg-protocol-announce-strategic-partnership-deliver-unified-global-standards
- **New timeline (29 Jul 2026):** the Corporate Standard, Scope 2, Scope 3 and Actions and Market Instruments workstreams will be **consolidated with ISO 14064-1 into one joint corporate standard**. The public consultation is in **Q2 2027** and publication in **Q4 2028**. Any transition period is still to be decided. https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Consolidated-StandardDevelopmentPlan(SDP)-2026.07.29.pdf ; https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/ghg-protocol-announces-key-standard-development-updates-faq-resource ; https://trellis.net/article/ghg-protocol-adjusts-standards-update-timelines/ (secondary)
  - **CONFLICT (superseded):** GHG Protocol pages from 2025 say "second Scope 2 consultation in 2026, final standard 2027", and some secondary sources still repeat it. The 29 Jul 2026 plan folds Scope 2 into the consolidated Q2 2027 consultation and Q4 2028 publication. Whether a stand-alone Scope 2 standard will still appear in 2027 is **UNVERIFIED**, and the sources found suggest it will not.
- **Scope 3 Phase 1 progress update (31 Mar 2026):** covers data quality, boundary setting, investments and a possible new category. Everything in it is draft. https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/S3-Phase1ProgressUpdate-20260331.pdf

### 2.11 CSDDD changes in Omnibus I

- **Original act:** Directive (EU) 2024/1760, published in the OJ on 5 Jul 2024 and in force since 25 Jul 2024. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1760/oj/eng ; https://commission.europa.eu/business-economy-euro/doing-business-eu/sustainability-due-diligence-responsible-business/corporate-sustainability-due-diligence_en
- **Scope:** more than **5,000 employees and more than €1.5bn net turnover** for EU companies, and the same turnover threshold in the EU for non-EU companies. https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251211IPR32164/simplified-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-rules-for-businesses (2025-12); Consilium 2026-02-24
- **Timing:** transposition by **26 Jul 2028**, and companies comply from **26 Jul 2029** in a single phase, replacing the old three-phase rollout. Consilium 2026-02-24 (above); https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/business-and-human-rights-insights/2026/02/omnibus-i-the-european-union-concludes-csddd-and-csrd-reforms.html (secondary)
- **Climate transition plans:** the CSDDD duty to adopt and put into effect a transition plan was **deleted**. CSRD-scope companies still *report* on a transition plan if they have one. https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251211IPR32164/simplified-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-rules-for-businesses ; Clifford Chance (secondary)
- **Civil liability:** the EU-harmonised civil liability regime was **removed**, and national rules apply. Clifford Chance and Covington (secondary): https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/02/eu-csddd-csrd-omnibus-published-in-official-journal-transposition-delegated-acts-and-guidelines-are-next
- **Penalties:** the maximum is **3% of net worldwide turnover**, replacing a *minimum* cap of 5%. The Commission will issue penalty guidelines. Clifford Chance and Covington (secondary)
- **Risk-based approach:** companies do a **scoping exercise** based on reasonably available information to find where adverse impacts are most likely and most severe. This covers direct **and indirect** business partners. The Commission proposal to limit it to direct (tier-1) suppliers was **not** kept. Where risks are equal, companies may prioritise direct partners. https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/06/23/simplification-council-agrees-position-on-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-requirements-to-boost-eu-competitiveness/ ; https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251222-eu-sustainability-omnibus-i-detailed-omnibus (secondary); https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/omnibus-i-clarity-on-the-future-of-the-csrd-and-csddd (secondary)
  - **CONFLICT:** Accountancy Europe's Mar 2025 explainer says "value chain due diligence limited to direct suppliers". That described the *proposal*, not the final text. https://accountancyeurope.eu/publications/omnibus-explained-key-changes-to-csddd/
- **Information requests:** requests to business partners with **fewer than 5,000 employees** are a last resort, for information that cannot reasonably be obtained another way. (secondary: Stibbe, Norton Rose Fulbright FAQs https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/231dbeb5/faqs-the-eu-corporate-sustainability-due-diligence-directive-cs3d-after-the-omnibus-revisions)
- **Guidelines and review:** the first CSDDD guidelines are due by **Jul 2027**, and the review clause runs to **Jul 2031** (secondary: Covington, above). The harmonisation clause was widened (secondary).

## 5. Common misconceptions to avoid

| Misconception | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| "CSRD was scrapped." | It was narrowed, not repealed. Large companies above 1,000 employees and €450m still report, wave 1 has reported since FY2024, and wave 2 starts with FY2027. (Consilium 2026-02-24; EC CSR page) |
| "Omnibus is still just a proposal." | It is law: Directive (EU) 2026/470, in the OJ on 26 Feb 2026 and in force since 18 Mar 2026. It still needs national transposition by 19 Mar 2027. |
| "The simplified ESRS are still a draft." | They were adopted on 3 Jul 2026 and published on 21 Sep 2026 as DR 2026/1563. Mandatory from FY2027 and optional for FY2026. |
| "Double materiality was dropped." | It was kept in the revised ESRS. (secondary, see 2.3) |
| "The threshold is 250 employees" or "500 employees". | 250 was the original CSRD large-company test, and 500 is the NFRD / wave-1 criterion. Today's test is **>1,000 employees and >€450m** turnover, both required. |
| "The threshold is 1,000 employees *or* €450m." | Both conditions must be met ("and"). (EP press release 2025-12) |
| "Listed SMEs must report from 2028." | Listed SMEs were removed from mandatory scope. |
| "SMEs must use the VSME." | The VSME is voluntary. It is a Commission *recommendation*, and the value-chain standard (DR 2026/1560) is for *voluntary* use. What changed is that it caps what reporting customers can demand from suppliers with up to 1,000 employees. |
| "Reasonable assurance is coming in 2028." | That pathway was removed. Assurance stays limited, and the EU limited-assurance standard is due by 1 Jul 2027. |
| "CSDDD requires a Paris-aligned transition plan to be implemented." | The CSDDD transition-plan duty was deleted. CSRD reporting on a plan *if one exists* remains. |
| "CSDDD only covers tier-1 suppliers." | That was the Commission proposal. The final text uses a risk-based scoping across direct *and* indirect partners, with permission to prioritise direct partners. (secondary, see 2.11) |
| "CSDDD applies from 2027." | That was the original three-phase schedule, later moved by stop-the-clock. Now: transposition by 26 Jul 2028, application from 26 Jul 2029. |
| "The Green Claims Directive is in force" or "was formally withdrawn." | Neither, per the latest sources found. It is stalled but formally pending. The rules that *do* apply from 27 Sep 2026 come from Directive 2024/825. |
| "The new GHG Protocol Scope 2 standard (hourly matching) applies from 2027." | Hourly matching and deliverability are consultation proposals. The current plan is a consolidated GHG Protocol/ISO standard, with consultation in Q2 2027 and publication in Q4 2028. Today the 2015 Scope 2 Guidance applies. |
| "Market-based Scope 2 replaces location-based." | The 2015 guidance requires both. The 2025 proposal keeps dual reporting. |
| "ISSB = ESRS" or "The EU adopted ISSB." | They are separate frameworks designed to be interoperable. The EU uses ESRS, and ISSB adoption is jurisdiction by jurisdiction. |
| "X countries have made ISSB mandatory" (a single hard number). | Counts vary by definition and date: IFRS Foundation Jun 2025 gives 36 (adopted or in progress), S&P Global Apr 2026 gives 28 adopted plus 12 planned. Cite source and date. |
| "Germany passed its CSRD law." / "Germany has no CSRD law" as a settled fact. | The latest verified step is the Bundestag hearing on 13 Apr 2026. Check the BMJV procedure page before stating either. |
| "AI can produce a compliant ESRS report." | No source supports this. Reports need management judgement (materiality), traceable data, and external limited assurance. Present AI as an aid for drafting, extraction and data checks, with human review. (This is workshop framing, not a regulatory claim.) |

## 7. Notes for Workshop 04 design (derived from the facts above; not regulatory claims)

- **Anchor the demo in public, stable structures.** Use the VSME Basic Module (11 disclosures, Scope 1 and 2), GHG Protocol scopes, and dual Scope 2 reporting. These are published and unlikely to change before the session, unlike the national German law.
- **Good "raw input → insight" exercises:**
  - Utility bills to Scope 2 location-based and market-based figures, with the difference explained.
  - A supplier questionnaire, checked against what may be demanded under the value-chain cap.
  - Marketing copy screened against the Directive 2024/825 claim bans, which apply from 27 Sep 2026.
- **Put a "rules as of <date>" footer on every regulatory slide**, and add a "check before you present" box for: German transposition, the Green Claims status, and the GHG Protocol timeline.
- **Avoid:** implying AI output is audit-ready; quoting single ISSB adoption numbers without source and date; "carbon neutral" examples that rely on offsets.
