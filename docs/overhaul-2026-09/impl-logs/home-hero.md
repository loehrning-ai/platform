# home-hero: phone home hero with the horizon globe (change log)

2026-09-26. Winner: proto-horizon, with the grafts and every mustFix from both judges. Scripts, screenshots and measurements: `scratchpad/mobile/home-hero/` (shots/ has final-*, handover-*, open-*, drag-*, full2.png, zoom200-*).

## What the phone sees (below lg)

One graphit band between the compact top bar and the tab bar (`min-height: calc(100svh - var(--nav-h-compact) - var(--tabbar-band-h) - 1px)`, never a fixed height):
1. The label "Frei, zweisprachig und quelloffen." It is hidden below 700px viewport height.
2. The full two-line H1, "KI verstehen. / Sicher anwenden." The H1 is static and is the LCP element.
3. One supporting sentence.
4. The horizon globe. Its apex sits about 40–47px under the lead at every phone size. It is a line globe seen from orbit:
   - Europe's coastlines drawn as hairlines with a depth fade.
   - Germany as a 1.5px Mennige outline with a 0.12 fill, and Berlin as a square station.
   - A tapered pole glint and a degree scale along the limb.
   - No halo and no coloured glow.
5. On the ground, in the thumb zone: the paper CTA "Lernroute wählen", and to its right a 44px pause control that appears only once the live renderer is running.
6. The continue row ("Erster Schritt · Claude Course"), docked as the band's last row directly above the tab bar. This is Option B. The row is restyled to Werkzeichnung and is still exactly 76px.

**Motion:**
- **CSS opening.** It runs at first paint, lasts 3.3s, runs once, and only under `no-preference`. The limb draws out from the apex, the lines open radially from Germany, Germany traces last, and one light sweep runs along the horizon. Every animation ends in its natural state (fill-mode backwards).
- **Canvas renderer.** It takes over after load, an idle callback, and the end of the opening. It draws the identical frame first, then hides the SVG. Handover diff: mean abs luma 1.4; mean luminance 23.28 (SVG) vs 23.59 (canvas).
- **Drift.** It eases into about 3°/s. A horizontal drag spins the globe with inertia. Vertical swipes scroll the page natively (`touch-action: pan-y`).

## Files

New:
- `src/components/werk/horizon-projection.ts`: shared pure math and constants. Composition ratios, view basis, decoder, polyline tracer, depth-fade stops.
- `src/components/werk/horizon-globe-frame.tsx`: **server component**, the SSR first frame. It reuses `globe-geometry.ts` for `graticulePath` and `GERMANY_OUTLINE`. Size: 11.2KB raw / 4.1KB gzip, no ids, three aria-hidden SVGs.
- `src/components/werk/horizon-globe-renderer.ts`: Canvas 2D renderer, dynamic-imported. 11.6KB gzip including the land data.
- `src/components/home/phone-globe.tsx`: the loader hook and the pause button. About 1.2KB gzip in the hero's client graph.
- `src/components/home/phone-hero.css`: band layout, tokens and opening. Every rule is scoped to `width < 64rem` and unlayered, so the rules outrank the desktop utilities without `!important`. The one exception is the `letter-spacing` override against the H1's inline style.
- `src/lib/horizon-land.ts`: generated by `scripts/extract-horizon-land.mjs` from world-atlas `land-50m`. 162 rings, 4,063 points, DP 0.25°, 7.1KB gzip. The licence is noted in the repo-root `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; the ISC texts already exist in `LICENSES/`.
