## What happens
1. The labour inspector, sometimes two (labour and safety), comes at 09:00 under notice 22-0487/1 of 2026-11-23 with three checklists: labour, safety at work, records.
2. You may go with the inspector, get a copy of each checklist and add a reasoned comment (ZIN Art. 54, 65).
3. The minutes are written on the spot and signed by both sides (ZIN Art. 65).
4. Papers the inspector asks for in writing are due within 3 calendar days of the request, or by the time the inspector sets (ZIN Art. 53(1), 55). Write the date of each request on templates/clock.csv.
5. Documents taken away: at most 15 days, and only against a receipt (ZIN Art. 52(3)). Ask for the receipt.
6. The act comes at the latest 8 days after the minutes (ZIN Art. 68). An appeal runs 15 days from receipt and does not stop the act (Art. 70).

## What you do not sign
1. Minutes that do not match the facts. You may refuse and give your reason in writing (ZIN Art. 54, 65).
2. Any paper with a date other than the day it is signed. No backdating, no change to an old record.
3. The drafts in drafts/ for someone else. Each one counts only when the named owner dates and signs it.
4. A receipt that does not list each document taken.
