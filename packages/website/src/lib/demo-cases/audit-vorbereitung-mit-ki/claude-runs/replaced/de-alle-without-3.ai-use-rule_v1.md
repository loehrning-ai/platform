Fictional training material.
Approved 2026-10-01 by Dragan Ilievski, Managing Director. Owner: Goran Petrovski, IT lead. Valid from 2026-10-01. Next review: 2027-10-01.

Applies to all staff of Vardar Works.
1. The only AI tool for customer data is our Claude Team workspace (25 seats, in use since 2026-09-01). Its data is stored in the US: see vendor-page_claude_2026-06-15.md.
2. Customer data never goes into a personal account or a free chatbot, on a laptop or on a phone.
3. Before you paste a service request, replace the customer's name, address and phone number with [NAME], [ADDRESS] and [PHONE].
4. No passwords, keys or contract files in any chat.
5. Questions go to the IT lead.
