# Release rule – item 2210 lentil soup 500 ml fresh

Checked every Sunday after the forecast run, on the last 13 valid weeks
(weeks noted "sold out" and weeks without a stored forecast don't count).
Baseline: "same as last week".

The forecast goes into planning without sign-off only if all four check lights are green:

1. **Data:** last week's actual and this week's forecast are both available.
2. **Baseline:** the forecast's mean absolute error is smaller than the error of "same as last week".
3. **Bias:** the sum of (forecast − actual) is no more than 5 % of the sum of actuals, too high or too low.
4. **Range:** the actual fell inside the 80 % range (10 %–90 % of the last 26 errors) at least 9 times out of 13.

If any light is red, **[person – to be named]** decides and records the number and the reason in the log.

A person always decides, whatever the lights show, when there is a new product, a customer
joins or leaves (for example the delisting on 7 April 2025), a price change of more than [10 %],
or a supply shortage.
