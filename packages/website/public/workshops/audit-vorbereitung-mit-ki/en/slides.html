  3. Example, not in the kit

    firewall-review.txt

    fails SCOPE The request asks for every site; one was reviewed.

  4. Example, not in the kit

    supplier-list.csv

    Supplier list. Owner: Purchasing. Approved by: ____

    APPROVAL

    Policy, contract: a name with a date inside the file. Record, register: who keeps it.

    fails APPROVAL Nobody approved it.

A file is evidence only when its quote, period, scope and approval hold. I give Claude those four tests as rules, and nothing counts without a verbatim quote.

The tool

  1. QUOTE · PERIOD · SCOPE · APPROVAL
  2. COVERED, WEAK or GAP. A file out of date or unapproved: WEAK. A part without evidence, or the record shows “not met”: GAP. The weakest part counts.
  3. No quote, no COVERED. A policy shows intent; only a record shows practice.

In the kit: project-instructions.md, rules 4 to 9.

Where Claude goes wrong on this folder

Vardar Works and Tannwald are invented · exercise date: 9 Nov 202609 / 12

Each mistake has a rule in project-instructions.md that stops it.

A typical wrong line

  1. R8 · infosec-policy_v4_DRAFT.md · COVERED

    Rule 7 DRAFT, “(open)” or a blank field is no approval. The newest file is not the valid one.

  2. R7 · “Backups are restore-tested twice a year.” · COVERED

    Rule 4 A policy shows intent. When the item asks about practice, only a record counts.

  3. R5 · 26 staff trained

    Rule 11 Count in code. Remove duplicate rows first and say how many there were.

  4. R4 · per ISO 27001 A.12.3.1

    Rule 12 No standard or control number that is in no file.
