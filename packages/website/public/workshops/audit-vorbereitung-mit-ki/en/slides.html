The rules and the data ladder

  • AI sorts, compares, counts, checks and drafts.
  • A named person checks and signs.
  • AI never creates, changes or backdates a record.
  1. Level 4 real datareal rows sent to the AI, after four gates
  2. Level 3 AI writes, you runa script that counts overtime, run on your own laptop
  3. Level 2 invented rowsGradba-Pro's invented payroll rows
  4. Level 1 public texta law text, a blank form

Payroll with names. Which level?

Level 3: the AI writes the check, you run it on your own machine.

Level 4 needs four gates.
