The one rule

Four tests per file

  1. QUOTEA line shows it happened

    fire-drill.md

    Fire drill: planned for spring.

    Planned is not a record.

  2. PERIODfrom 10.12.2025

    visitor-policy.md

    Visitor policy. Approved 2023-05-02 by the Managing Director.

    Approved before the window.

  3. SCOPEevery system, every person

    firewall-review.txt

    Firewall review, site Skopje. Done 2026-06-03 by the IT lead.

    One site instead of all.

  4. APPROVALwho, when

    supplier-list.csv

    Supplier list. Owner: Purchasing. Approved by: ____

    Nobody approved it.

COVEREDall four hold

WEAKold or unapproved

GAPmissing or not met

project-instructions.md · rules 4 to 9

No quote, no COVERED. A policy shows intent; only a record shows practice.

A file is evidence only when its quote, period, scope and approval hold. I give Claude those four tests as rules, and nothing counts without a verbatim quote.

COVERED, WEAK or GAP. A file out of date or unapproved: WEAK. A part without evidence, or the record shows “not met”: GAP. The weakest part counts.
