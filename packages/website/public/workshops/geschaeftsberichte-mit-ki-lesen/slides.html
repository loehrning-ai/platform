The decision depends on the cards.

Counted by scrapped units and per shipping week
Rework CRAFT first. Hold the extra Q3 marketing for one month.
BecauseScrapped units: 168 per 1,000 units shipped, 123 in August. Demand per shipping week fell by 2.1 %.
Number1,539 scrapped units, €691,011 at the mean list price of €449, 16.8 % of CRAFT revenue. Valued at list price, not at cost.
RiskHold wrongly: a month of possible extra sales lost. Push wrongly: scrap grows with every extra unit sold, over six machines.
Counted by records and month total, as the report
More Q3 marketing for CRAFT.
BecauseDefects 3.21 to 2.51 per 1,000, units +22.4 %. The VP's request.

metric-cards.md · release condition

A trigger must not be met by paperwork alone.

The condition under which the extra marketing is released next month. Three checks before you write it into the card.

Trigger, by numeratorcounts units, not recordsnot met todayperiod and source named
Release when scrapped CRAFT units in October are below 80 per 1,000 units shipped.✓✓168 today✓
Release when affected CRAFT units in October are below 250 per 1,000 units shipped.✓✓309 today✓
Release when CRAFT is below 2.51 records per 1,000 units shipped in October.✗Met in September already by merging: 17 records give 1.86.✓2.51 today✓

80 is a little under half of September's 168. The threshold is a setting and the metric card says so.
