The AI Analyst · business reports

The recipe — any company, any month

1 · What do we track?Your handful of monthly metrics.
2 · Where does each number come from?The source file or system — and its honest limits.
3 · What does it mean HERE?Your definition (e.g. "revenue = list price, not margin").
4 · What can the data NOT answer?Name it instead of faking it.
5 · What decision does the month feed?The one call the report should help you make.

Answer these five and you've written a metrics Skill — for NORDA today, your company Monday. Rebuild from the blank your-company-skill/ template in your kit.

Prove it — run every time (not optional)
