Workshop 01 · Forecasting decisions

Turn a forecast into a decision

In about 90 minutes you work out what a wrong forecast costs, how big the buffer should be, and when a person has to approve. You work through three practice companies and a demand simulator that runs in your browser.

Open the hands-on lab Read the business case

About 90 minutes · self-study · runs in your browser · English

Materials

Four pages and one dataset, in this order

  1. 02Hands-on lab→Three labs: ZIP capacity for a parcel network, one shared number for a device maker, a release gate for a social app.
  2. 03Business case→One phone launch, three teams and a demand forecast that is higher than the stock.
  3. 04Field card→The checks, formulas and thresholds on one A4 page to print.
  4. 05Take-home→Build two forecasts on the dataset, compare their error and write down your service level.

Dataset for the take-home: demand-weekly.csv, 104 weeks of synthetic demand.

What you can do afterwards

  1. Check, in euros, whether a model beats the rule “same weekday last week”.
  2. Set the service level from the cost of too much and too little stock.
  3. Decide which signal stops the automation and who approves the restart.
  4. Write a go/no-go rule with a threshold and a named approver.
