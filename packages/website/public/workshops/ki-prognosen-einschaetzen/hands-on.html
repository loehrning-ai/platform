Parcel network
Teaching lens | Last-mile ZIP capacity

Set tomorrow's ZIP capacity

What we predictTomorrow's package demand for a ZIP cluster — to size delivery capacity (vans, drivers, shifts).
Manual todayA planner types “same weekday last week” into a spreadsheet.
AI advantageLearns the weekday rhythm and the promo calendar, so it catches the demand spike the sheet misses — cutting the cost of over- and under-building. The one shock no model can see is flagged to a human.
ObjectiveSee, in dollars, where AI beats the manual rule — and where it correctly hands the call to a person.
Device maker
Teaching lens | Demand planning to logistics to resellers

Stop the laptop bullwhip

What we predictWeekly laptop demand, shared as ONE number down the chain (demand planning → logistics → store).
Manual todayEach tier adds its own safety buffer on top of the padded order beneath it.
AI advantageOne shared, calibrated forecast replaces stacked local buffers — collapsing the bullwhip amplification while holding service and freeing the cash tied in inventory.
ObjectiveSee how a single shared signal beats local padding on bullwhip, cost and fill rate.
Social app
Teaching lens | Live demand sensing and capacity release

Gate fast demand before it ships

What we predictFast-moving user demand, nowcast live and released behind a trust gate.
Manual todayShip the deploy-day plan and don't look again (no-gate auto-release).
AI advantageA monitored model detects drift, routes the exception to an owner, retrains, and resumes — avoiding the capacity loss the blind plan bleeds.
ObjectiveSee why the value is a governed release LOOP, not a prettier forecast line.
