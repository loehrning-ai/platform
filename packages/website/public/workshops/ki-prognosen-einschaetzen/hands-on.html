Parcel network
Last-mile ZIP capacity

Set tomorrow's ZIP capacity

What we predictTomorrow's package demand for a ZIP cluster, to size delivery capacity (vans, drivers, shifts).
Manual todayA planner types “same weekday last week” into a spreadsheet.
What the model addsIt learns the weekday rhythm and the promo calendar, so it catches the promo spike the sheet misses and builds less excess capacity. The one shock no model can see goes to a person.
GoalSee in euros where the model beats the manual rule, and where it hands the call to a person.
Device maker
Demand planning, logistics, resellers

Stop the laptop bullwhip

What we predictWeekly laptop demand, shared as one number down the chain (demand planning → logistics → store).
Manual todayEach tier adds its own safety buffer on top of the padded order beneath it.
What the model addsOne shared, calibrated forecast replaces the stacked local buffers. The bullwhip shrinks, service holds, and less cash sits in inventory.
GoalSee how a single shared signal beats local padding on bullwhip, cost and fill rate.
Social app
Live demand and capacity release

Gate fast demand before it ships

What we predictFast-moving user demand, nowcast live and released behind a trust gate.
Manual todayShip the deploy-day plan and never check it again (auto-release without a gate).
What the model addsA monitored model detects drift, sends the exception to an owner, retrains and resumes. That avoids the capacity the unchecked plan loses.
GoalSee where the money comes from: the release gate and its owner.
