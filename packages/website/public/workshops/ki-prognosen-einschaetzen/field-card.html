Workshop 01 · Forecasting decisions

Field card

1Newsvendor critical ratio (CR)

service level = CR = Cu / (Cu + Co)

Cu is the margin lost per unit short, Co the unit cost minus salvage per leftover.

If Cu > Co, CR is above 50%, so order more than the average. Bakery: Cu≈4.1, Co≈1.3, CR≈0.76, bake to 76%. Gadgets: CR≈0.84, stock to 84%.

2Order more or less?

More when margins are fat, stockouts hurt, goods keep or resupply is slow.
Less when margins are thin, goods spoil, restocking is fast or storage is costly.

3Safety stock

safety stock = z · σ · √(lead time)

z by service level: 50% 0.00 · 76% 0.70 · 90% 1.28 · 95% 1.65 · 99% 2.33.

Before you trust a forecast

  1. Beat “same as last week” or drop it.
  2. Pick the model by its backtest score.
  3. Report a range with every forecast.
  4. Test on unseen weeks from several starts.
  5. Write down what it cannot cover: novelty, regime change, reflexivity, one-off events.
