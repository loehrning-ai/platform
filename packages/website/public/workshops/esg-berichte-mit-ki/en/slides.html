Appendix 1 · Sources

Sources

  • Directive (EU) 2024/825Annex I points 2a, 4a, 4b, 4c, 10a: always banned. Applies since 27.09.2026, in Germany through the UWG.
  • BGH, judgment of 27.06.2024, I ZR 98/23Whoever advertises with “climate neutral” explains in the advertisement itself whether reduction or offsetting is meant.
  • IHK on the third act amending the UWG of 12.02.2026Applies since 27.09.2026. Newly banned: an environmental claim that rests on offsetting greenhouse gases.
  • EBRD, press release of 18.12.2025Up to EUR 8 million to Sparkasse Bank Skopje, lent on to SMEs for renewable energy and energy efficiency (Green Finance Facility).
  • Vinmonopolet, weight requirement for glass bottlesSince 01.01.2026: still wine under NOK 250 in bottles of at most 420 g per 0.75 l. A requirement for suppliers.
  • Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560VSME, the EU's voluntary standard for SMEs, since 24.09.2026. Basic Module B1 to B11.
  • Directive (EU) 2026/470Value-chain cap, financial years from 1.1.2027 (appendix 2).

Summarised in files/green_claims_rules.md and files/vsme_basic_module.md. A learning aid, not legal advice.
