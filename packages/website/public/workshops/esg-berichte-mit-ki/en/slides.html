Appendix 1 · Sources

Sources

  • Directive 2005/29/EC, Annex IPoints 2a, 4a, 4b, 4c, 10a (inserted by 2024/825): always banned. Since 27.09.2026, in Germany through the UWG.
  • BGH, judgment of 27.06.2024, I ZR 98/23Whoever advertises with “climate neutral” must explain in the advertisement itself whether reduction or offsetting is meant.
  • IHK on the third act amending the UWG of 12.02.2026Applies since 27.09.2026. Newly banned: “climate neutral”, “reduced” or “positive” based on offsetting.
  • EBRD, press release of 18.12.2025Up to EUR 8 million to Sparkasse Bank Skopje, lent on to SMEs for renewable energy and energy efficiency (Green Finance Facility).
  • Vinmonopolet, weight requirement for glass bottlesSince 01.01.2026: still wine under NOK 250 in bottles of at most 420 g per 0.75 l. A requirement for suppliers.
  • Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560Voluntary Standard (VS, formerly VSME), in force since 24.09.2026, for financial years from 2027. Basic Module B1 to B11.
  • Directive (EU) 2026/470Value-chain cap, financial years from 1.1.2027 (appendix 2).

Summarised in the folder. A learning aid, not legal advice.

What customers may ask for from 2027

vsme_basic_module.md
  1. 35Value-chain cap: for financial years from 1.1.2027, companies with up to 1,000 employees can refuse
  2. 36questions from reporting customers, for the customer's sustainability report, that go beyond the
  3. 37datapoints in Annex II of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560 (Directive (EU) 2026/470).
  4. 38Requests under other EU or national rules (such as banking supervision, due diligence) are
  5. 39not capped. For the reporting year 2025 the cap does not apply yet.

It does not apply here: Nordvik asks for a tender (questionnaire l. 5), not for its own report (l. 36), and for 2025 (l. 39).
