What is true on 26 September 2026

  1. 1Fewer companies must report

    Since 18 March 2026 (Directive (EU) 2026/470) the CSRD at EU level covers companies with more than 1,000 employees and more than €450m net turnover; both must be met. National law by 19 March 2027. Kellbrunn has never been in scope.

    Council press release, 24 Feb 2026 · EUR-Lex 2026/470

  2. 2The standards got shorter

    Revised ESRS were published on 21 September 2026 (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563): mandatory for financial years from 1 January 2027, optional for 2026.

    Commission, 3 Jul 2026 · OJ, 21 Sep 2026

  3. 3Customers can ask, within a cap

    Directive 2026/470 lets suppliers with up to 1,000 employees refuse requests beyond the voluntary standard (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560). Transposition by 19 March 2027. Check whether 2026/1560 keeps the Scope 1 and 2 datapoints.

    EP press release, Dec 2025 · EUR-Lex 2026/1560

  4. 4VSME Basic Module: Scope 1 and 2

    EFRAG VSME, December 2024: 11 disclosures, including energy and Scope 1 and 2; no double materiality assessment, no assurance required. Which Scope 2 methods B3 requires: check the EFRAG text.

    EFRAG

  5. 5Scope 2 is reported twice

    The 2015 GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance asks for location-based and market-based, and it still applies. A joint GHG Protocol and ISO standard is planned: consultation Q2 2027, publication Q4 2028.

    Scope 2 Guidance · GHG Protocol update, 29 Jul 2026

  6. 6Green-claim rules from 27 Sep 2026

    Directive (EU) 2024/825 bans, towards consumers, generic environmental claims unless recognised excellent environmental performance can be shown, and product climate claims based on offsetting. Who a text is aimed at is for a lawyer to judge. Misleading claims to business customers are banned too (§ 5 UWG).

    EUR-Lex 2024/825 · Commission FAQ · UWG route secondary
