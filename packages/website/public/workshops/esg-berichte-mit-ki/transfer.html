Workshop 04 · ESG Reporting with AI · Transfer sheet

One bill, five boxes

Use an invented or anonymised bill. No company data goes into any tool.

Prints on one A4 page. Write in the dashed boxes; the worked example is Kellbrunn's Werk Süd electricity.Prints on one A4 page, with space to write next to each box. The worked example is Kellbrunn's Werk Süd electricity.

  1. 1Source

    File, page and the exact line you read.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    Jahresuebersicht_2025_Oekostrom.md, page 1, “Verbrauch 2025: 1.240 MWh”

  2. 2Period

    From, to, months covered; gaps or overlaps with other bills.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    01.01.2025 to 31.12.2025, 12 months, no overlap. Last year on the same page: 1.250 MWh.

  3. 3Unit

    Value and unit as printed → normalised value, and the rule.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    1.240 MWh → 1,240,000 kWh (MWh × 1,000; the dot separates thousands). Check: 22,545 kWh per employee, close to last year's 22,727 kWh.

  4. 4Boundary

    Legal entity on the document; in or out; which rule.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    Kellbrunn Präzisionsteile GmbH, Werk Süd; in; operational control.

  5. 5Factor

    Factor ID, year, basis, edition; method; any certificate.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    Location-based: F-EL-LB-2025 (0.40, illustrative teaching value) → 496.0 t.

    Market-based: F-EL-GO under I-WS-GO (1,240 MWh guarantees of origin, cancelled, Werk Süd only) → 0.0 t.

End with one sentence

“This number comes from [document, line], covers [period], was converted by [rule], belongs to [entity] under [boundary rule], and uses [factor ID, year, edition]. Still open: [one thing].”

„Diese Zahl stammt aus [Beleg, Zeile], deckt [Zeitraum] ab, wurde mit [Regel] umgerechnet, gehört zu [Gesellschaft] nach [Regel zur Bilanzgrenze] und nutzt [Faktor-ID, Jahr, Stand]. Noch offen: [eine Sache].“

Space to write your sentence

Worked sentence

This number comes from the Werk Süd annual statement 2025, line “Verbrauch 2025: 1.240 MWh”, covers January to December 2025, was converted with MWh × 1,000, belongs to Kellbrunn Präzisionsteile GmbH, Werk Süd, under operational control, and uses F-EL-LB-2025 (edition Lehrwerte v1.0, 2026-01-15). Still open: the monthly split, if the customer asks for quarters.
