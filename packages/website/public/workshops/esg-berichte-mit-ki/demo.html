Which trap moves the total, and by how much?

2 of 6 · Trap switches

Every switch starts at “Fixed”. Set one to “As the AI did it” and the total, the distance from the right answer and the change against 2024 update. Each total is looked up from the 128 precomputed combinations of the traps, for the Scope 2 method you pick. Six traps change the location-based total. The seventh, T7, only touches the market-based figure.

Guided sequence6 steps, about 6 minutes

  1. Predict: which switch moves the total most? Duplicate March, Talbrück bill or “1.240 MWh”.

    Your prediction

    The MWh misread moves it most: −495.5 t on its own, location-based. Talbrück alone adds +400.0 t, the duplicate +82.0 t.

  2. Set only the Talbrück bill (T3) to “As the AI did it”.

    2,315.2 t · +14.8% vs 2024.

    Why it matters +14.8% against 2024 makes anyone ask what happened, so this error gets caught.

  3. Also set the MWh misread (T4) to “As the AI did it”.

    1,819.7 t · −9.8% vs 2024.

    Why it matters A fall of 9.8% looks plausible again, so nobody asks.

  4. Switch the method to market-based. The MWh row now shows 0 t.

    2,493.2 t · −6.8% vs 2024 (market-based).

    Why it matters Market-based hides the unit error completely, because the certificate prices Werk Süd's power at zero. A fall of 6.8% looks fine with the Talbrück bill still inside.

  5. Switch back to location-based, then set only the duplicate March (T1) and October (T2) to “As the AI did it”.

    1,917.2 t · −5.0% vs 2024.

    Why it matters Together they move the total by only +2.0 t, so a check on the total misses both. In market-based the pair moves it by +3.0 t.

  6. Open the evidence drawer from “72.2 t” in the rewritten sentence in section 4.

    Why it matters In this case's teaching values, 71% of the location-based decrease comes from the lower grid factor. Kellbrunn did nothing for it.

What made the total fall against 2024?

4 of 6 · Drivers

Both bridges share one axis that starts at 1,700 t, not at zero. The change bars are to scale; the 2024 and 2025 bars are shortened.

Consumption effects use the 2024 factor; the factor effect uses 2025 consumption. The residual mix is held at 0.60 in both years on purpose, so the market-based bridge has no factor effect. Real residual mixes change every year.

The rewritten sentence. Every number opens its driver line and rows.

Scope 1 and 2 fell location-based (). In this case's teaching values, of that comes from a lower grid factor and from using less electricity, gas and diesel. Market-based fell ; of that is guarantees of origin covering Werk Süd since January 2025.

The AI's sentence: “The reduction comes mainly from efficiency measures at Werk Nord.” The folder has no production volumes, so “used less” is supported and “more efficient” is not.

The capture protocol
  1. Hold the model, prompt, factor file, 2024 summary and date constant.
  2. Conditions: A: raw folder; B: ledger + coverage grid + rules; C (optional): raw folder without the factor file.
  3. 5 runs per condition, each scored on 13 items, from Scope 1 to whether it asks about other Scope 1 sources.
