Which trap moves the total, and by how much?

2 of 6 · Trap switches

Every switch starts at “Fixed”. Set one to “As the AI did it” and the total, the distance from the right answer and the change against 2024 update. Each total is looked up from the 128 precomputed combinations of the traps, for the Scope 2 method you pick.

Guided sequence6 steps, about 6 minutes

  1. Predict: which switch moves the total most? Duplicate March, Talbrück bill or "1.240 MWh".

    Your prediction

    2,315.2 t · +14.8% vs 2024.

What made the total fall against 2024?

4 of 6 · Drivers

Consumption effects use the 2024 factor; the factor effect uses 2025 consumption. The residual mix is held at 0.60 in both years on purpose, so the market-based bridge has no factor effect. Real residual mixes change every year.

The rewritten sentence. Every number opens its driver line and rows.

Scope 1 and 2 fell location-based (). In this case's teaching values, of that comes from a lower grid factor and from using less electricity, gas and diesel. Market-based fell ; of that is guarantees of origin covering Werk Süd since January 2025.

The AI's sentence: “The reduction comes mainly from efficiency measures at Werk Nord.” The folder has no production volumes, so “used less” is supported and “more efficient” is not.

The capture protocol
  1. Hold the model, prompt, factor file, 2024 summary and date constant.
  2. Conditions: A: raw folder; B: ledger + coverage grid + rules; C (optional): raw folder without the factor file.
  3. 5 runs per condition, each scored on 13 items, from Scope 1 to whether it asks about other Scope 1 sources.
