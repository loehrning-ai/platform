# The ESG folder: what belongs in it and what you check on each document

One folder per reporting year. Check when you file a document, not when the request arrives.

| Document | VSME | Period | Scope | Final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company profile: legal form, NACE, turnover, balance sheet total, employees, sites | B1 | annual accounts | all sites | accounts approved |
| Electricity invoices | B3 | 12 distinct months, nothing booked twice | all meters | invoice, not an instalment |
| Supply mix statement from the supplier | B3 | reporting year | all supply contracts | from the supplier |
| Fuel receipts in litres (diesel, heating oil, gas) | B3 | reporting year | all vehicles, machines, heating | receipts, no estimate |
| Grid emission factor | B3 | year stated | country | published, source named |
| Environmental permit or check of the reporting duty | B4 | valid on the reference date | site | signed |
| Map check of the sites against protected areas | B5 | dated | all sites | map source named |
| Water invoices, plus wells or other sources | B6 | 12 months | all sources | invoice or meter |
| Waste records: total, hazardous, recovered | B7 | reporting year | all waste types | record from the waste company |
| Staff: totals by contract type and gender | B8 | annual average | employees only | payroll |
| Accident register | B9 | reporting year | employees only | register |
| Hours worked | B9 | reporting year | employees only | payroll |
| Statement on minimum wage, share under collective agreements, training hours | B10 | reporting year | all employees | signed |
| Statement on convictions and fines | B11 | names the years | company | signed, dated |
| Certificates | B1 | valid from, valid until | read the scope | original or copy |
| Audit reports | B1 | date, next audit | area audited | no seal, no certificate |
| Policies (environment, corruption) | B2 | date adopted | what they cover | adopted |

Not in the folder: requests, drafts, unsigned offers, offset certificates as proof of own emissions,
files with the names of individual employees.

If a document is missing, a note with its name goes into the folder, never an estimated value.
