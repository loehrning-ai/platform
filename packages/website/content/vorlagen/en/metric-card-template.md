# Metric cards

For a team meeting: one block per metric, every line filled in. A metric
without a card is not used in a decision paper.

A metric means only what its card says. Pin down numerator, denominator and
period, trace one figure to its rows, then decide.

## Four forks, go through them before you fill in

| Fork | Question for the room |
|---|---|
| Events or quantities | Do we count reports, tickets, orders, or units, euros, hours? |
| Absolute or per base | Does the number grow just because the business grows? |
| Month total or per week | Do the periods we compare hold the same number of weeks and working days? |
| Reported or adjusted | What do we take out, and does it come back in other periods? |
