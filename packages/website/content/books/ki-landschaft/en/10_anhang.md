# Appendix: terms, sources, and limitations

## Glossary

| Term                   | Definition                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI Act**             | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 with obligations based on role, system, and risk.                                                                   |
| **Provider**           | Actor under Art. 3 No. 3 AI Act; classification follows the actual conduct and can change under Art. 25.                                      |
| **Deployer**           | Actor under Art. 3 No. 4 AI Act that uses an AI system under its own authority, except in the course of a personal non-professional activity. |
| **Data flow**          | Documented movement of data among people, devices, services, regions, subprocessors, logs, and backups.                                       |
| **DPIA**               | Data protection impact assessment under Art. 35 GDPR when processing is likely to result in a high risk.                                      |
| **FRIA**               | Fundamental rights impact assessment under Art. 27 AI Act for the group of addressees and high-risk deployment specified there.               |
| **High-risk AI**       | AI systems that fall under Art. 6 with Annex I or III; exceptions and the specific intended purpose require assessment.                       |
| **AI literacy**        | Skills, knowledge, and understanding under Art. 3 No. 56; Art. 4 requires context-appropriate measures, not a specific certificate.           |
| **Human oversight**    | The organizational and technical ability of competent, authorized people to understand, monitor, override, or stop a system.                  |
| **Pilot**              | Limited, measurable test with defined data, users, controls, stop criteria, and evaluation.                                                   |
| **Technical exposure** | Overlap between tasks and technical system capabilities; not equivalent to adoption, automation, or job displacement.                         |

Representative survey of 604 companies in Germany with at least 20 employees; the results do not automatically apply to microenterprises below that threshold.

## Claims removed or not used

This book does not use a private company database, a proprietary scoring engine, regional or sector-specific company rankings, or a competitive analysis. Percentages, samples, signal weights, and maturity scores derived from such sources are not part of the open learning materials.

Market forecasts or product rankings from commercial secondary sources are also excluded when no robust primary source and clear method are available.

## Limitations

1. US labor-market exposure must not be presented as a German forecast of adoption or employment.
2. Company surveys measure reported use or assessments, not necessarily technical effectiveness.
3. Public web features do not reliably show internal processes, data quality, or controls.
4. Product features, prices, contracts, data flows, and the legal position change. Volatile information requires a review date and a primary source.
5. Learning material does not replace a legal, data-protection, security, or professional assessment of the specific use.
