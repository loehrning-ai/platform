## What the title does not say

Of {{h02-gap.ads}} ads that name LLM (large language models, the technology behind chatbots) or generative AI in the text, {{h02-gap.no_ai_title}} (interval {{h02-gap.no_ai_title.range}}) have no AI word in the title, {{h02-gap.no_ai_title_linkedin}} on LinkedIn and {{h02-gap.no_ai_title_indeed}} on Indeed. Anyone who counts only titles misses them. A keyword in the text shows a wish of the firm, not a filled job.

If you count only titles, there are {{h02-ladder.ki_title_ratio}} ads for students for every junior ad in the AI field, and if you read the text as well it is {{h02-ladder.ki_text_ratio}}.

> **What you can do:** Read the text of an ad and not its title, and count distinct jobs instead of ads before you believe a number from the job market. If you advertise yourself, name the task a language model should take over in one sentence, and the name of the tool only if you use it.
