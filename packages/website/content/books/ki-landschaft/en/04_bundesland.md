# Assess regional conditions

The managing director of a metal shop in Thuringia is no worse off than her peer in Bavaria. A registered office produces no AI readiness. Differences do exist: funding programs, advisory services, university networks, sector clusters, administrative practice.

## No ranking without representative data

Public websites, company domains, and provider addresses do not represent internal systems and skills. A ranking of the federal states by "AI readiness" does not follow from them.

Name the decision that needs regional information instead.

## Funding

Funding programs change. Check before you apply or commission:

- the current funding guidelines and program term
- eligible company size and place of business
- eligible expenses and exclusions
- whether the application must be submitted before the project starts
- rules on combining grants and state-aid requirements
- evidence, retention, and disbursement conditions

Use the [Förderdatenbank des Bundes](https://www.foerderdatenbank.de/) and the responsible state development bank as primary sources. Do not trust a static program list printed in a book.

## Supervision and advice

For data protection questions, find the responsible independent supervisory authority. Under the AI Act, national and sectoral market-surveillance responsibilities can join in. Chambers, Mittelstand-Digital-Zentren, and universities give orientation. They do not replace a binding assessment of the individual case.

## Skills and partners

Compare partners by evidence, not by distance or marketing rank:

- documented experience in the specific process and sector
- a security and data protection plan
- reproducible tests and clear acceptance criteria
- transfer of knowledge to your own team
- exit provisions, documentation, and no unnecessary dependency
- a transparent scope of services and costs

## Infrastructure

Regional broadband and data centers can count. Cloud region, registered office, and data residency are three different things. Check the specific service, its contracts, subprocessors, configuration, and data flows.

Location is a condition. System purpose, data, controls, and operations decide the use case.
