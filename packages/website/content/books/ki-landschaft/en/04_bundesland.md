# Assess regional conditions

No reliable conclusion about AI readiness follows from a company's registered office. Germany's federal states differ, however, in funding programs, advisory services, university networks, sector clusters, and administrative practice.

## No ranking without representative data

Public websites, company domains, and provider addresses do not represent internal systems and skills. They therefore cannot support a sound ranking of Germany's federal states by "AI readiness."

State the decision that requires regional information instead.

## Funding

Funding programs change. Before applying for funding or commissioning a project, check:

- the current funding guidelines and program term
- eligible company size and place of business
- eligible expenses and exclusions
- whether the application must be submitted before the project starts
- rules on combining grants and state-aid requirements
- evidence, retention, and disbursement conditions

Use the [Förderdatenbank des Bundes](https://www.foerderdatenbank.de/) and the relevant state development bank as primary sources. Do not rely on a static list of programs in a book.

## Supervision and advice

For data protection questions, identify the responsible independent data protection supervisory authority. National and sectoral market-surveillance responsibilities may be relevant under the AI Act. Chambers, Mittelstand-Digital-Zentren, and universities can provide orientation, but they do not replace a binding assessment of an individual case.

## Skills and partners

Compare partners by evidence, not by distance or marketing claims:

- documented experience in the specific process and sector
- a security and data protection plan
- reproducible tests and clear acceptance criteria
- transfer of knowledge to the internal team
- exit provisions, documentation, and avoidance of unnecessary dependency
- a transparent scope of services and costs

## Infrastructure

Regional broadband or data-center services may be relevant. Cloud region, registered office, and data residency are distinct concepts. Check the specific service, its contracts, subprocessors, configuration, and data flows.

Location is a contextual factor. System purpose, data, controls, and operations determine the use case.
