The interval widens when there are only a few comparable firms, and the calculation runs in your browser, with no data leaving it. The value is an estimate.

In my data, text volume is more strongly associated with the AI word than industry or state. The estimator is calculated only with websites the rescan read, without those whose legal notice names another firm. For many of them the legal notice leaves open whether it belongs to the firm, so read industry and state with caution. Size counts authorised representatives, not employees. It describes what is visible on a homepage, not your use.

## What has applied since February 2025

Since 2 February 2025, Article 4 of the EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, has applied. The amending Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force since late July 2026, has recast the duty, and providers and deployers take measures that support the development of AI literacy of their staff and of other persons who work with AI systems on their behalf. According to the Commission's answers, the Regulation does not require proof of attendance, a fixed training course or a measurement of individual employees' knowledge. This is not legal advice. For a decision, read [Regulation 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R1689) and the [amendment 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R1744) on EUR-Lex as well as the [questions and answers of the Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers).

## Three days and one process

At a former employer, the answer to the question “Where does our data live?” was: in Excel sheets on network drives. You could work with that. A start is knowing where your material lives and which AI already runs in your firm, including as a function in programs that have long been in use. That is my suggestion and not a proven procedure. Plan three days in which you answer the following questions with the people who use the system every day.

- Which AI already runs here, including where nobody calls it that?
- What is it used for, with which data, and whom does the result affect?
- Who needs what to work with it safely?

After that you take a single process and measure it before and after with your own numbers.

1. Choose a process that repeats, has a clear owner and has a result you can check.
2. Measure it for two weeks beforehand: start, end and how often the result has to be corrected.
3. Write down how you will recognise success before you begin.
4. Let the tool work for four weeks under supervision, with one person who checks every result and counts every error.
5. Compare with the measurement from before and decide: continue, change or stop.

A negative result counts too, if you measured it. Keep customer data out of any tool whose contract you have not read during this time.

> **What you can do:** Pick the one process this week and begin the measurement beforehand. Write down how many hours it costs today and how you will see in four weeks that something has changed. Also decide who checks the results and by when.
