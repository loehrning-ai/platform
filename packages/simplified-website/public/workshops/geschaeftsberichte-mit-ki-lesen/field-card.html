The four prompts (in order)

1 · Ground itRead the backstory and both reports. Who is NORDA and what decision is this month's report building toward?
2 · Compute (run the Skill)Use the norda-metrics skill: run its script, show revenue & defects by line and the three things that matter. Don't estimate what the script can compute.
3 · Build the dashboardWrite dashboard/data.js from metrics.json, then open dashboard/index.html.
4 · Make the callRework CRAFT or reallocate its budget? Give the number, the cost of being wrong, and what the data can't tell us.
