# Checklist excerpt: Labour inspection

Invented paraphrase of the public inspectorate checklist. The rule sentence is what the check reads.

| line_id | article | question | rule_sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | ZRO Art. 15(2),(3) | Is there a written contract for every worker, kept at the seat, with a copy handed over? | Every worker has a written contract kept at the seat, and the worker got a copy. |
| L02 | ZRO Art. 13(3),(7) | Was the M1/M2 form filed at the latest the day before each worker started? | The M1/M2 form is filed at the latest one day before the worker starts. |
| L04 | ZRO Art. 116(8) | Does the attendance sheet show the start and the end of work for every working day? | Up to 25 employees: an attendance sheet shows the start and the end of work for every working day. |
| L05 | ZRO Art. 117(2) | Does overtime stay at 8 hours a week or less? | Overtime is at most 8 hours a week. Order picking can be organised in shifts, so the exception does not apply (kit sentence, invented). |
| L06 | ZRO Art. 117(6) (offence listed in Art. 265(1)) | Is there a written notice to the inspector for every overtime period? | For every overtime period a written notice to the labour inspector exists (kit test: it names the person who signed it). |
| L07 | ZRO Art. 117(5), 110(2) | Do overtime hours have their own line in the pay calculation? | Overtime hours appear on their own line in the pay calculation, so overtime can be seen (kit sentence, invented). |
| L08 | ZRO Art. 109(2),(3) | Was pay paid within 15 days after each pay period, with a free day moving the limit? | Pay is paid at the latest 15 days after the end of the pay period; a free day moves it to the next working day. |
| L09 | ZRO Art. 137(1),(4) | Does every worker with 6 months of service have a written decision on annual leave of at least 20 working days? | Every worker with 6 months of service has a written decision on the right to annual leave of at least 20 working days. |
