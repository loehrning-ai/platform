# AI acceptable use policy, version 1.2
Fictional training data. Every company, person and document is invented.

Owner: Head of Operations. Approved: 2025-06-18. Next review: 2026-06-18.

1. Approved tools: the Claude Team workspace (drafts and summaries of content without patient data) and the self-hosted triage model (ticket sorting).
2. Patient data (names, dates of birth, contact data, visit reasons, lab values) may only be processed by the self-hosted triage model. The model runs in our EU environment, so the data does not leave the EU.
3. Never paste patient data into the Claude workspace or into any other external AI tool.
4. Every employee completes security and AI training once a year.
5. A person checks every AI output before it goes to a customer.
6. A new AI tool needs the approval of the Head of Operations.
