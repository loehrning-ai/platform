Fictional training material, invented for the exercise. Not legal advice.
A file is evidence only when its quote, period, scope and approval hold. I give Claude those four tests as rules, and nothing counts without a verbatim quote.
COVERED, WEAK or GAP. A file out of date or unapproved: WEAK. A part without evidence, or the record shows “not met”: GAP. The weakest part counts.
No quote, no COVERED. A policy shows intent; only a record shows practice.

## Step 1

Predict first, then check ANSWERS.md.
In the room 5 minutes. At your desk about 20, our estimate, not measured.

    Read the three checklist excerpts. Make a table with one row per checklist question: line id, article, the paper that answers it, its owner, where it lives (use folder-index.md). Do not open the folder files yet. If a paper is not in the files, write NOT IN FILES. Use ids, no names.

## Step 2

Predict first, then check ANSWERS.md.
In the room 7 minutes. At your desk about 20, our estimate, not measured.

    Write and run a check on the CSV, txt and md files in the folder. In the CSV files skip the lines that start with #. Print how many rows you read in each file. For every finding print the id, the rule and the number: filing later than the day before the first shift; overtime weeks above 8 hours; overtime weeks without a notice; pay lines without an overtime line; attendance rows without start and end; pay later than 15 days after the period (a free day moves it); leave decisions missing or under 20 days (owed after 6 months); empty record fields; exams older than 24 months on 27 Nov 2026; job titles missing from the safety statement; training given later than the hire date. Then list every near miss you checked and ruled out, with the reason. Change no file.

No code tool in your plan? Ask Claude for the check as a script, for example check.py, and run it on your own computer with python3 check.py folder/ (Windows: py check.py folder). Real firm: take the script and run it on your own machine; send the tool only column names and 3 invented rows. To practise that, use templates/sample-rows_attendance.csv.

## Step 3

Predict first, then check ANSWERS.md.
In the room 6 minutes. At your desk about 30, our estimate, not measured. Do it in two goes: the statuses and the new papers first, then the briefing, the notes and the files.

    Use the table and the findings. Give every question COVERED, WEAK or GAP with the four tests and a quote. For each WEAK and GAP name the new paper that closes it (never a change to an old one), the owner by role and the day; today is 26 Nov 2026. Fill templates/evidence-index.csv and write templates/clock.csv from the notice (working files, not records). Write one page for the person who will be present on 27 Nov: what happens, what they answer and what they do not sign. Draft the new paper notes for E-11 and E-19 (leave) and for the overtime notice from templates/new-paper-note.md. Leave every signature line empty.
