# Safety card: what goes in, what never, who signs

## What may go in

1. Public text, such as a law or a blank form.
2. Invented rows, such as this kit.
3. Real files stay on your machine: Claude writes the script, you run it, and only column names and 3 invented rows go to Claude.
4. Real rows only after four gates: a business subscription, a data processing contract, security measures and rules for sending data abroad. Ask your data protection adviser.

## What never goes in

- The national ID number (EMBG). The law says it must not be shown or printed without need.
- Health content. Dates only, for example the date of the work fitness exam.
- A thumbs up or down on a chat with real rows. The rating stores the whole chat for up to 5 years, also on business plans. A Team owner can switch rating off.

## Good to know

- Team, Enterprise and API: chats are not used to train Claude by default. Free, Pro and Max: you choose, under Settings, Privacy.
- Claude's servers are in the US. Since the 2025 amendment the transfer rules do not apply to NATO members such as the US, but the firm still notifies the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZLP) on its Form No. 1.
- Anthropic's Usage Policy (version effective 12 November 2026) forbids falsified employment records. It treats HR decisions about a worker as high risk: a qualified person stays accountable.

## Who signs

AI sorts, compares, counts, checks and drafts. It never creates, changes or backdates a record. A named person checks and signs.

Sources, checked 8 November 2026: Personal Data Protection Law (consolidated text with SV 101/25), AZLP Form No. 1, Anthropic privacy centre and Usage Policy.
