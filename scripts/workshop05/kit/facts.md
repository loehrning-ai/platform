# Facts: Claude plans and the EU AI Act

Checked 18 Oct 2026. Vendor pages change: re-read before you rely on this. Not legal advice. Format: statement | source | date.

1. Free, Pro, Max follow the Consumer Terms. Team, Enterprise follow the Commercial Terms. | Anthropic privacy 10023580, 7996868 | 2026-03-16, 2026-08-18
2. On Free, Pro, Max training is a setting you control. The page does not state the default: look at yours. | Anthropic privacy 12109829 | 2026-08-03
3. Team and Enterprise chats are not used for training by default. Thumbs feedback is an exception. | Anthropic privacy 7996868 | 2026-08-18
4. A deleted chat leaves your history now and the back end within 30 days, and is not used for future training. With training on, new chats can be kept de-identified for up to 5 years. The page does not say if deleting shortens that. | Anthropic privacy 10023548 | 2026-07-01
5. Team: saved chats have no fixed period stated. Deleted chats go within 30 days. | Anthropic privacy 7996866 | 2026-07-01
6. Enterprise adds an owner delete timer (minimum 30 days), audit logs, a Compliance API, SCIM. | Anthropic help 10440198, 9797531 | undated, 2026-09-01
7. Chat data is stored in the US. Processing may run in the US, Europe, Asia and Australia. The page offers no EU-only option. | Anthropic privacy 7996890 | 2026-06-15
8. On Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud you pick the region with the endpoint, so an EU region can be chosen. That is the partner's hosting and contract, not the Claude apps. | platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/data-residency | undated, read 2026-10-18
9. The Desktop app is a window onto your cloud account. With Cowork you connect folders. In cloud sessions, files Claude opens are processed on Anthropic's servers. Admins can switch Cowork off and limit it. This is our reading: check the vendor page. | support.claude.com/en/articles/14479288 | undated, read 2026-10-18
10. A personal plan has no company admin tools and no company contract. | Derived from the plan pages on claude.com/pricing | read 2026-10-18
11. AI literacy duty since 2 Feb 2025. Since 27 Jul 2026: take measures to support staff AI skills; no specific level, no certificate needed. Banned since 2 Feb 2025: reading staff emotions from voice, face or body data at work (medical or safety reasons excepted). | EU AI Act; Regulation (EU) 2026/1744; Commission Q&A | 2026-07-24, 2026-07-27
12. Since 2 Aug 2026: tell people when they deal with AI, label deepfakes, and label unreviewed AI text on public-interest matters. Internal email and ordinary marketing text are not what the text rule targets (our reading; a deepfake is covered anywhere). Chatbot: check your role. | EU AI Act transparency rules; Commission FAQ | 2026-08-07
13. From 2 Dec 2027: high-risk uses such as ranking applicants or rating staff (moved from 2 Aug 2026). A normal chat assistant for drafting or summarising is not high-risk. | Regulation (EU) 2026/1744; Commission FAQ | 2026-07-24, 2026-08-07
14. A firm outside the EU can be covered when the AI's output is used in the EU. | EU AI Act (unofficial mirror) | undated, read 2026-10-18
15. GDPR still applies: a legal reason, and a contract with the vendor who handles the data. | GDPR (unofficial mirror) | undated, read 2026-10-18
