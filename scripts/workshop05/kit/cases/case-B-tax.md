# Field card B: tax control (UJP)

Laws as of 7 November 2026. Sources are named at the end, read on 2026-11-07. The kit holds no data for this case.

## Who comes and how you learn the date
A tax inspector of a regional directorate of the Public Revenue Office (UJP), external control department. For cash registers an inspector or controller, often a team of two. ZIN does not apply to UJP (ZIN Art. 2), so the ZIN notice, checklist and once-a-year rule are not rights here.
- The control starts with a written order. The notice is two weeks for most taxpayers and four weeks for large ones, except where the notice would prevent or hinder the control (ZDP Art. 92). Then the order is handed over right before the start.
- A cash register check starts with the order handed over right before the check.
- The start can be postponed on request for illness, renovation or force majeure.

## The clocks
- Notice: 2 weeks, or none.
- Length: no maximum, only "the necessary time" (ZDP Art. 95(3)). The calendar length of a control is not published.
- Requests for information: UJP sets the time for each request (Art. 60(1)).
- Lawsuit: within 30 days of service (ZUS Art. 26(1)). It does not stop enforcement (ZDP Art. 176(2)).

## The five papers asked first
Books and accounting documents, invoices in and out with the VAT records, annual accounts, payroll records, and for a cash register: fiscal receipts, the daily report book and the record of takings paid in.

## Bottlenecks
Sourced: missing or unreliable books are punished twice, by a fine and by an estimate that is not in favour of the taxpayer (ZDP Art. 76). UJP may inspect existing data on devices that hold a storage medium, make a faithful copy and rebuild changed and deleted data (ZDP Art. 60-a(1),(2)).
This kit's story (invented): cash register data and books that live with an outside accountant.
Not measured: the calendar length of a control.

## Cost and what the law leaves open
- ZDP fines, legal entity: EUR 300 to 1,000 (micro) up to EUR 3,000 to 10,000 (large); responsible person EUR 50 to 500. ZRGP Art. 21b: EUR 600 to 1,000 (micro), 1,500 to 2,000 (small), 3,000 to 6,000 (medium), 4,000 to 10,000 (large). Size thresholds unverified.
- UJP 2025: 3,888 payment orders issued and 3,332 paid; the average order was about 24,500 MKD (derived). Interest example: 100,000 MKD of VAT unpaid for a year costs about 10,950 MKD at 0.03 percent a day (derived).
- Sealing a shop: no fiscal system, 30 days; no receipt, until the fine is paid, 15 days at most.
- Press, medium confidence: in one week (28 Jul to 3 Aug 2025) the tax authority checked 214 businesses, mostly in resort towns, and found irregularities at 66.
- Survey year 2023, World Bank Enterprise Survey: 51.5 percent of 354 formal firms with 5 or more employees were visited or met tax officials. A meeting is not an audit.
- Accountant fee: price lists of single vendors only, not a market average.

## How to use AI well and legally
AI may sort, compare, count, check and draft. Reconcile the three numbers UJP compares: register daily totals, bank deposits, the VAT return. Match bank lines to invoices and list lines with no paper. Turn the order into a document checklist with owners. Draft the comment on the minutes for the accountant to sign. A named person checks and signs. No real personal data goes into a tool the company does not control: never paste payroll lists into a consumer chatbot.

## Fix forward
AI never creates, changes, completes or backdates a record. ZDP Art. 47(2): books and records, also in electronic form, must not be changed so that the original content can no longer be determined. Criminal Code Art. 280 can apply to false, inaccurate or incomplete entries in required books: prison 1 to 5 years for a person, a fine for a legal entity. Art. 378 can also apply (medium confidence). Write a new paper with today's date instead.
