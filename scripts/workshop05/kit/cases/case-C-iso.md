# Field card C: ISO 9001 audits and customer audits

Sources are named at the end, read on 2026-11-07. The kit holds no data for this case.

## Who comes and how you learn the date
An audit team of a certification body, named in advance so that you can object. A customer audit comes from the customer's own auditor. Dates of ordinary audits are agreed in advance. Short-notice or unannounced audits exist, for example after a complaint (third-party summary, medium confidence).

## The clocks
- Audit days of the initial audit only (IAF MD 5:2023), by effective personnel, not plain headcount: 3 days for 16 to 25 staff, 4 days for 26 to 45, 5 days for 46 to 65.
- Yearly check about one third of that time, recertification about two thirds.
- A major nonconformity not verified as closed within 6 months after the last day of Stage 2 means Stage 2 is repeated (summary of a paid standard, medium confidence).
- Automotive customers (IATF only): no response to a major nonconformity within 15 calendar days of the closing meeting means a failed audit. Not every customer works this way.

## The five papers asked first
Quality policy, quality objectives, process map, document control, corrective actions. The internal audit and the management review follow.

## Bottlenecks
Sourced: where it jams are document control, corrective actions without a root cause, lapsed calibration, missing competence records and a thin management review (certification practice, Sept 2026, medium confidence). The auditor must not suggest the cause or the solution.
This kit's story (invented): the dates of ordinary audits are agreed in advance, so the work is in the files, not in a notice period.

## Cost and what the law leaves open
- One Skopje consultancy (about 10 staff, VAT status unknown), advertisement, ISO 9001:2015, May 2024: certification audit about EUR 1,200, first yearly check about EUR 600, implementation EUR 800 to 1,000. This is an advertised price of one consultancy, not a certifier's tariff and not a market average. There is no price for 24 staff.
- Note, medium confidence: ISO 9001:2026 was announced as published on 16 September 2026, with a transition to 30 September 2029 (a certification body's news item).
- We found no reliable count of ISO 9001 holders in North Macedonia, and no number for the lost business after a failed audit.

## How to use AI well and legally
AI may sort, compare, count, check and draft. Build the evidence index: clause or question, document, version, owner, date. Compare document versions and find outdated procedures. Plan the internal audit 6 to 8 weeks before the auditor comes. Draft root-cause text that the process owner confirms. Draft answers to a customer questionnaire with the document and page named in every yes. A named person checks and signs. No real personal data goes into a tool the company does not control. ISO says its standards must not be uploaded to AI tools unless ISO licenses it.

## Fix forward
AI never creates, changes, completes or backdates a record, such as calibration, training, internal audit or management review. Such records can fall under Criminal Code Art. 378 (medium confidence). Never answer yes to a questionnaire for a control the firm does not run. Never present AI text as approved by the auditor. Write a new paper with today's date instead.
