# Field card A: labour inspection (DIT)

Laws as of 7 November 2026. Sources are named at the end, read on 2026-11-07. Everything about Gradba-Pro is invented.

## Who comes and how you learn the date
A state labour inspector of the State Labour Inspectorate (DIT), sometimes two, one for labour and one for safety. ZIN, the general inspection law (SV 135/2025), applies from 1 January 2026. The inspectorate also has its own special law, so the notice period and other rules can differ in a real case. Ask an adviser.
- A regular inspection is announced by a written notice with the checklist at least three working days before it starts (ZIN Art. 57(2),(3)). There are exceptions where special laws allow it or where immediate action is needed.
- An extraordinary inspection is by rule unannounced and starts within 10 working days of the initiative or written order (ZIN Art. 58).
- You name one employee who is present. You may accompany the inspector, get a copy of each checklist, add a reasoned comment and refuse to sign minutes that do not match the facts. The minutes are written on the spot and signed by both sides (ZIN Art. 54, 65).

## The clocks
- Notice to visit: at least 3 working days for a regular inspection.
- Papers the inspector asks for: within 3 calendar days of the request (ZIN Art. 53(1)). For the firm's own documents the inspector can set the time in the request (Art. 55).
- Act: at the latest 8 days after the day the minutes are drawn up (Art. 68).
- Appeal: within 15 days of receipt. It does not stop the act (Art. 70).
- Documents may be taken for at most 15 days against a receipt (Art. 52(3)).
Whether the day of the notice and the day of the visit count is not settled in our sources.

## The five papers asked first
Employment contracts, attendance sheets, overtime notices to the labour inspector, pay calculations with the overtime line, leave decisions. With a safety inspector the safety statement and the medical exam papers follow.

## Bottlenecks
Sourced: the clocks are short and fixed (3, 3, 8 and 15 days). Late or inaccurate documents are themselves a fineable offence (ZIN Art. 83). DIT annual report for 2025, proposal version, published April 2026: 124 inspectors and 26,484 inspections (labour and safety), so about 214 inspections for each inspector (derived).
This kit's story (invented): papers kept in several places, attendance sheets that are only ticks, overtime notices that were never written, a safety statement older than the hall. Nothing in this second list is a measured finding.

## Cost and what the law leaves open
- Fines of ZIN Art. 83 for data not delivered in time, no access, nobody present, inaccurate data: a legal entity pays EUR 500 to 1,000 (micro), 1,500 to 2,000 (small), 3,000 to 6,000 (medium), 5,000 to 10,000 (large); the responsible person EUR 250 to 500. The size class of Gradba-Pro is assumed. The inspector decides.
- Labour decisions of DIT in 2025: 3,192, of them 2,316 warnings, 849 orders and 27 bans, so 73 percent were warnings. Paying a labour fine within 8 days of issue halves it; this does not apply to a safety fine.
- Safety fines: ZBZR Art. 58(6) names a court for them, not the inspector (read in a secondary copy of the law). A release of the inspectorate of 22 October 2024 (old law, building sites) also reports 32 safety payment orders in one day. So ask an adviser who imposes a safety fine today.
- Left open on purpose: whether real hours match the sheets, the minimum wage, the overtime bonus amount, supplements, the safety decree, the annual overtime cap. Staff time and lost sales: no source.

## How to use AI well and legally
AI may sort, compare, count, check and draft. Turn the public checklist into an evidence index. Check your own files with a script: registration date against the first shift, weekly overtime against the cap, pay date against the 15-day rule, leave against the 20-day minimum, exam dates against 24 months. Draft the clock, the briefing page and the comment on the minutes. A named person checks and signs. No real personal data goes into a tool the company does not control.

## Fix forward
AI never creates, changes, completes or backdates a record. Write a new paper with today's date and leave the old one as it is. Records changed afterwards can fall under Criminal Code Art. 280 or Art. 378 (can apply, not will).
