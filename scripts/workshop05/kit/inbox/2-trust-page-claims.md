# Draft claims for the new Trust page and the tender
Fictional training data. Every company, person and document is invented.

From: the sales lead. To: security. Marketing wants these seven sentences on the website and in the tender for a German hospital group. Which ones can we keep?

1. Patient data never leaves the EU.
2. Termin Health is ISO 27001 certified.
3. Our ticket-sorting AI runs in an EU data centre, so patient data stays in Europe.
4. We do not train or fine-tune any model on patient data.
5. All our staff are trained in data protection and in AI use.
6. Every admin account is protected with multi-factor authentication.
7. We back up daily, encrypt the backups and test a restore twice a year.
