# Erasure request: sampled by the auditor
Fictional training data. Every company, person and document is invented.

Rheinland Praxisverbund AG will pick one erasure request from 2026 and follow it through your systems. This is the one they picked.

- 2026-09-02: the clinic Köln-Mitte tells Termin Health that patient P-48213 asks for erasure.
- 2026-09-21: Termin Health confirms in writing: "All data of patient P-48213 has been deleted."

Task: trace patient P-48213 through every place that could hold their data, and say whether the confirmation was true on 21 September 2026.
Audit rule: the trail must show, for each place, the data, the country and the proof of deletion.
