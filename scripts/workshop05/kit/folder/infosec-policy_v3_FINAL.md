# Information security policy, version 3 FINAL
Fictional training material.
Approved 2024-03-12 by Dragan Ilievski, Managing Director. Next review: March 2025.

1. Every system with customer data has a named owner.
2. Administrator access is reviewed every quarter and the review is recorded.
3. Backups are restore-tested twice a year.
4. All staff complete data protection training every year.
5. Incidents with customer data are reported to the customer within 48 hours.
