# Cost per jar, lentil soup 500 ml (invented)

- Price to the shops: €3.10. Cost to make: €1.60.
- The soup is cooked on Monday for the week. When the stock runs out, the shops get
  nothing more until Monday. A missing jar costs the contribution margin:
  €3.10 − €1.60 = €1.50.
- Whatever is left on Sunday evening, a staff canteen collects for €1.20 a jar.
  A leftover jar costs €1.60 − €1.20 = €0.40.
- Without the canteen a leftover jar costs the full €1.60.
- Not included: annoyance in the shop about an empty shelf. If you put a euro value
  on it, raise the shortage cost and write down the reason.
