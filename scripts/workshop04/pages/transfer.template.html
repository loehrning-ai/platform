Workshop 04 · ESG Reporting with AI · Transfer sheet

One bill, five boxes

Use an invented or anonymised bill. No company data goes into any tool.

Prints on one A4 page. Write in the dashed boxes; the worked example is Kellbrunn's Werk Süd electricity.

  1. {{sec}}Source

    File, page and the exact line you read.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    {{j:traceTwo.0.file}}, page {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.source_page}}, “{{j:traceTwo.0.quote}}”

  2. {{sec}}Period

    From, to, months covered; gaps or overlaps with other bills.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.period_start:dmy}} to {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.period_end:dmy}}, {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.months}} months, no overlap. Last year on the same page: {{j:inputs.ws.priorYearPrinted}}.

  3. {{sec}}Unit

    Value and unit as printed → normalised value, and the rule.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    {{j:inputs.ws.printed}} → {{n:el_ws_kwh}} (MWh × 1,000; the dot separates thousands). Check: {{n:ws_kwh_per_employee}} per employee, close to last year's {{n:ws_2024_kwh_per_employee}}.

  4. {{sec}}Boundary

    Legal entity on the document; in or out; which rule.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.entity_on_document}}; in; operational control.

  5. {{sec}}Factor

    Factor ID, year, basis, edition; method; any certificate.

    Space to write

    Worked example

    Location-based: {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.factor_lb}} ({{j:factors.F-EL-LB-2025.value:fix2}}, teaching value) → {{n:ws_lb_2025}}.

    Market-based: {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.factor_mb}} under {{j:ledger.E-WS-01.instrument_id}} ({{n:go_mwh}} guarantees of origin, cancelled, Werk Süd only) → {{j:factors.F-EL-GO.value:fix1}} t.

End with one sentence

“This number comes from [document, line], covers [period], was converted by [rule], belongs to [entity] under [boundary rule], and uses [factor ID, year, edition]. Still open: [one thing].”

„Diese Zahl stammt aus [Beleg, Zeile], deckt [Zeitraum] ab, wurde mit [Regel] umgerechnet, gehört zu [Gesellschaft] nach [Regel zur Bilanzgrenze] und nutzt [Faktor-ID, Jahr, Stand]. Noch offen: [eine Sache].“
